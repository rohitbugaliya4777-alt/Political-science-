# Political-science-
Academic notes on Political SciencePolitical Science
Political Science is the systematic study of the state, government, political institutions, power, political behaviour, rights, duties, and relationships between citizens and the government.
It helps us understand how a country is governed, how laws are made, how political decisions are taken, and how power is distributed in society.
1. Major branches of Political Science
1. Political Theory
Studies concepts such as justice, liberty, equality, rights, democracy and sovereignty.
2. Comparative Politics
Compares political systems, constitutions and governments of different countries.
3. International Relations
Studies relations between countries, diplomacy, war, peace, international organisations and foreign policy.
